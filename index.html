About Me

I am a driven and creative individual with a passion for technology, leadership, and the arts. As a student of Cebu Institute of Technology – University and an officer of the Computer Students’ Society, I actively engage in opportunities that strengthen both my technical skills and my ability to lead and serve others.

My journey is shaped by discipline, creativity, and a commitment to excellence. I have been recognized as a Regional Festival of Talents (RFOT) qualifier and have received distinctions such as Outstanding Performance in Arts and Special Recognition for Service.

Beyond academics and competitions, I express my passion through movement and performance as a proud member of the Don Sergio Dance Troupe.

“Growth is a continuous journey—use your skills, embrace your passion, and create impact wherever you go.”
